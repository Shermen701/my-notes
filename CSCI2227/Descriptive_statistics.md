### Measure of center
mean: average
median: middle value
mode: most frequent value

### Measure of spread
range: max - min
interquartile range (IQR): Q3 - Q1
variance: average of squared differences from the mean
standard deviation: square root of variance

### Measure of position
percentile: k-th percentile is a value below which k% of the data falls
quartile: first quartile (Q1) is the 25th percentile, second quartile (Q2) is the 50th percentile (median), third quartile (Q3) is the 75th percentile
z-score: standardized score that tells how many standard deviations a value is from the mean
