### Sampling methods
- random sampling: every individual in the population has an equal chance of being selected
- systematic sampling: select every k-th individual from the population
- convenience sampling: select individuals who are easily accessible
- stratified sampling: divide the population into subgroups and select a random sample from each subgroup
- cluster sampling: divide the population into clusters and select a random sample of clusters
