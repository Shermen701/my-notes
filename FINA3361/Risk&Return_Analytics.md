## Expected Returns
$$E(R) = \sum_{i=1}^n R_i \times P(R_i)$$

### Variance
$$\sigma^2(R) = \sum_{i=1}^n (R_i - E(R))^2 \times P(R_i)$$

### Standard Deviation
$$\sigma(R) = \sqrt{\sum_{i=1}^n (R_i - E(R))^2 \times P(R_i)}$$


## Risk
Total Risk = Systematic Risk + Unsystematic Risk
Total Risk = market Risk + Idiosyncratic Risk


### Systematic Risk: Beta
$$\beta_i = \frac{\operatorname{Cov}(R_i, R_m)}{\operatorname{Var}(R_m)}$$
Beta measures sensitivity of stock's return to market's return  
Beta of market = 1  
Beta > 1: aggressive security  
Beta < 1: defensive security  

### Risk free Asset
0 Beta  
0 std dev  
0 Correlation with any asset



### Diversification
decline of unsystematic risk as portfolio grows  
well diversified portfolio: unsystematic risk approx 0  
total risk = market risk only
